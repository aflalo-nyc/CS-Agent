LESSONS FROM TEAM REVIEW — binding on every draft, newest review last.
(Maintained by `aflalo-cs learn` from the Airtable 'Comments + Feedback' column; last updated 2026-09-01.)

- Answer only the question that was asked; do not volunteer price, duties, product descriptions, restock timing, or policy extras the customer did not request (LW, 2026-09-01).
- Keep replies to two or three short sentences whenever the answer is a single fact or link (LW, 2026-09-01).
- Round or simplify figures rather than citing hyper-precise numbers (say "over 230 countries," not "237 countries and territories") (LW, 2026-09-01).
- Cut explanatory asides and definitions the customer did not ask for, such as clarifying that a measurement is a flat garment measurement rather than a body measurement (LW, 2026-09-01).
- For sizing or measurement questions, give just the requested measurements with no styling, fabric, stock, or pricing commentary (LW, 2026-09-01).
- Hyperlink the tracking number itself rather than pasting a bare tracking URL (LW, 2026-09-01).
- Embed links on descriptive text (for example "this link") instead of exposing raw URLs in the body (LW, 2026-09-01).
- For return how-to questions, reply with a thank-you, the returns portal link, and an offer to answer questions — nothing more (LW, 2026-09-01).
- For out-of-stock items, say the item is sold out and that the customer has been added to the waitlist to be notified when it returns; stop there (LW, 2026-09-01).
- Open damage replies by thanking the customer for flagging it, apologizing specifically for the defect, and noting it is not the quality standard we hold ourselves to (LW, 2026-09-01).
- State the action already taken (for example "I have gone ahead and made a return label for you") rather than offering the customer a menu of remedies to choose between (LW, 2026-09-01).
- When a next step needs customer input, ask one clear confirming question (for example the delivery address) at the end (LW, 2026-09-01).
- When pricing depends on destination, ask the customer to confirm the ship-to country first instead of quoting a figure with caveats (LW, 2026-09-01).
- Do not draft a reply to messages that need no response, such as a customer simply acknowledging a hold or a link (LW, 2026-09-01).
- Use a simple greeting with the customer's first name, a brief "Thanks for reaching out," and a short warm sign-off; avoid over-familiar or reassuring filler (LW, 2026-09-01).
