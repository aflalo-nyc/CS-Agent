# Call sheet: CS agent pricing: making quotes one final amount

The agent already quotes the correct product price per country, in local currency,
rounded, as a range when variants differ. To email one FINAL all-in figure, two facts
and one sign-off are missing:

1. When an international customer checks out, does the checkout price ALREADY include
   duties and import taxes, or are duties charged separately (e.g. on delivery)?
   (Shopify: Settings -> Markets -> the country -> Duties and import taxes.)
2. What do we charge for international shipping, per country? Flat, free over a
   threshold, or calculated? If a rate card exists, please share it.
3. OK for the email quote to be "product price + shipping, with duties per answer 1,
   final total at checkout"? This decides the exact sentence every price email uses.
